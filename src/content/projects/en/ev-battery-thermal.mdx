## Challenge

During **fast charging**, large temperature differences between cells accelerate uneven ageing. The goal was to keep the spread below 5 °C without a significant increase in coolant-loop pressure drop.

## Approach

- 3D **conjugate heat transfer (CHT)** model of cells, cold plate and coolant in Simcenter STAR-CCM+.
- Coolant loop and pump control strategy modelled in 1D with **Simcenter Amesim**, coupled to the 3D model.
- Three channel layouts evaluated: parallel, serpentine and branched.

## Outcome

The **branched channel** layout reduced the cell-to-cell temperature spread from about **8 °C to 4.2 °C** over the fast-charge cycle, with a negligible pressure-drop penalty.

## Lessons learned

Combining **detailed 3D with 1D system models** makes it possible to evaluate long duty cycles while keeping local accuracy — a key strength of the Simcenter portfolio.
