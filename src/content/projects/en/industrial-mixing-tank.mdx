## Challenge

A plant needed to shorten the **mixing time** of a 5 m³ batch tank and evaluate replacing the Rushton turbine with a **pitched-blade** impeller to cut motor power.

## Approach

- Four-baffle tank with the free surface modelled using **VOF** to capture vortex formation.
- Impeller modelled with mesh motion (**Rigid Body Motion**) to resolve the unsteady flow.
- Mixing time determined from a **passive scalar** tracer at monitoring points.

## Outcome

The pitched-blade impeller produced stronger axial circulation, **reducing mixing time by about 22%** and **impeller power by 10%** at the same speed. A dead zone near the tank bottom disappeared after lowering the impeller clearance.

## Lessons learned

For stirred tanks, **impeller clearance** matters as much as impeller type. A passive scalar is a cheap and effective way to quantify mixing quality.
